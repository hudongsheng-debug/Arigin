## How your data is processed

Arigin is a scientific graphing and data analysis application. Worksheet editing, plotting, fitting, statistics, and file conversion take place on your device. No account is required.

Arigin does not automatically upload worksheets, graphs, fit results, file contents, or file paths to the developer. It contains no advertising, advertising trackers, or third-party behavioral analytics services, and does not create profiles for tracking across apps or websites.

## Local files and preferences

When you open, import, save, or export a file, Arigin accesses the file locations you select or authorize through the system file interface. These files may contain worksheet values, column names and units, masks, graph styles, analysis records, and workspace settings.

Workspaces and exports are saved to locations you choose. Preferences such as interface language are stored locally. Changing the interface language does not translate or upload your data or titles.

If you save files in a folder with cloud synchronization, sharing, or backup enabled, those activities are managed by the system feature or service you choose. They are not uploads from Arigin to the developer.

## Clipboard

Arigin reads clipboard content when you invoke actions such as Paste, Reload Clipboard, or Paste Graph Style. It writes content to the system clipboard when you copy data, a figure, or a style. The application does not continuously monitor the clipboard in the background or send its contents to the developer.

## Information you send to support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any screenshots, logs, or sample files you choose to attach. This information is used to respond, investigate issues, and handle your request. It is not sold or used for advertising campaigns.

Please send a minimal example with personal information, confidential research, and third-party material removed. You do not need to submit a complete research dataset to ask a product question.

Support correspondence and attachments are retained only as needed to resolve the issue, conduct necessary follow-up, and meet applicable obligations. You may use the contact address above to request access, correction, or deletion of information you voluntarily provided to the developer. Requests will be handled as permitted by applicable law. Email transmission and storage are also subject to the policies of the relevant email service providers.

## Third-party and system services

Arigin does not provide developer-hosted data storage or cloud analysis. Store distribution, system diagnostics, device backups, file synchronization, and email services are handled by their respective providers under their own policies. Your device and service settings determine which system features you enable.

## Your choices

You decide whether to import files, copy or paste content, or contact support. You control the workspaces and exported files saved on your device or in your chosen folders and can move or delete them. Removing the application does not necessarily remove documents saved separately. Cloud copies and backups must also be managed through their respective services.

## Policy changes

If data practices change, this policy and its update date will be revised, along with store privacy information when necessary. For questions or privacy requests, contact Hu Dongsheng at hudongsheng356@gmail.com.
