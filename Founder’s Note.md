# 开发初衷 /  Founder’s Note

开发者 / Developer: 胡冬生 / Hu Dongsheng

为了让使用苹果电脑的人也能拥有熟悉、顺手的科学绘图与数据分析体验，我们开发了 Arigin。我们希望把整理数据、探索图形、拟合曲线和准备论文配图这些日常工作，放进一个清晰、专注的工作区。

很多研究者已经熟悉 Origin 中整理数据、绘图和拟合的工作方式。为了让使用苹果电脑的人也能拥有类似的工作流体验，我们开发了 Arigin，并围绕本地数据、清晰的界面和日常科研需求，逐步完善功能。

Arigin 为独立开发的应用，与 OriginLab 没有隶属、授权或背书关系，也不承诺完整复现 Origin 的功能或项目格式。

We built Arigin to give people using Apple computers a familiar, practical scientific graphing and analysis experience. Our aim is to bring everyday tasks—organizing data, exploring plots, fitting curves, and preparing research figures—into one clear, focused workspace.

Many researchers are familiar with the workflow of organizing data, plotting, and fitting in Origin. We built Arigin to offer a similar workflow experience to people using Apple computers, with a focus on local data, a clear interface, and everyday research tasks.

Arigin is independently developed and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by OriginLab. It does not promise complete reproduction of Origin’s features or project formats.
